What Is a Large Language Model? Term #1

"A next-word predictor wearing the costume of understanding"

Size
Core Architectural Truth

"Pattern completion, not comprehension"

An LLM does not possess a human-like comprehension module or reasoning mind. It is a high-speed mathematical predictor designed to calculate the most probable token following a given context sequence.

No understanding module inside

Zero inherent consciousness or semantic awareness.

Just very good prediction

Statistical patterns extracted from massive training sets.

Repeated, word by word

Autoregressive loops generating answers token by token.

Phase 01

Massive Reading

"Reads everything, understands nothing"
Phase 02

Pattern Memory

  • "Thank you for" seen millions of times
  • Usually followed by "your business"
  • Builds statistical instincts
  • No dictionary, just repetition
"Pure pattern completion"
Phase 03

Next Word Prediction

  • Picks most likely next word
  • One word at a time
  • Repeats thousands of times/sec
  • Full answer emerges
"Feels understood, isn't"
